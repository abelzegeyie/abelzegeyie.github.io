### Who is Abel X?

My name is Abel, but you can call me Abel X (or just X)—whatever you want, as it's good and easy for you. 

Currently, I am a Psychology student at Jimma University in the College of Education and Behavioral Sciences. This is the long walk I have on my hand. I am deeply interested in the study of human behavior, especially analytical psychology, with a strong motive to really know and understand how the mind works. Back in Grade 10, 11, and 12, I spent hours listening to motivational videos on YouTube, and I truly think those early years helped clean the road for the path I am walking today.

But my interests don't stop there; I am a guy who hustles across many fields, trying every day to be a disciplined man. I have a huge passion for programming, influenced heavily by my sister and my childhood home tries. That curiosity grew into working with things like Python, JavaScript, and UI/UX design to build real functional blueprints. 

Then there is the financial market. This area influenced me massively, starting with my early cryptocurrency activity. That paved the way for me to jump into forex trading. I have been in this industry for almost 4 years now—staying, learning, understanding market sessions, working on scaling funded accounts, and gaining real knowledge about market trends. I am interested in many, many areas, but for now, psychology, programming, and finance are my main ones. 

### The 1000 Days Journey ;)

I am active on social media—Facebook, sometimes TikTok, YouTube, but mostly Telegram, where I run the "Abel X chat" community. With my channel, I prepare journeys for us to take together. We do 30-day and 365-day (1 year) promises, like reading the Bible daily, building habits, doing something productive, blah blah. 

Seeing the community grow and pushing ourselves motivated me to be even more focused on my own effort. So, for further thinking and moving forward, I created another journey for the next 1000 days ;). 

This blog website is a major part of that journey! It has much more personal meaning and goals inside it, but I’ll leave those personal details for myself. But let me tell you the real public purpose of this website.

### What You Will Find Here

I made a promise to myself to share what I know, what I have learned, and what opportunities I have found for others—just as I told my Telegram community one time. So basically, depending on the topic, I will write in Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, or English as necessary. I will be sharing content across 3 main pillars:

*   **Psychology 101 (Fundamental):** My goal is to introduce the true science of psychology to all our people. Expect real-world psychological facts, general knowledge, and deep information about human behavior.
*   **The Financial World:** After almost 4 years in the trenches, I want to share the reality of this industry. I will break down trends, forex trading realities, and cryptocurrency insights so you can learn from what I've experienced.
*   **Real Lifestyles:** And by lifestyle, I mean *real* life—not what we know on social media like fake luxury life blah blah lol. I will be writing about technology, DIY projects, trend facts, book insights, and genuinely useful information you can use from social media.
