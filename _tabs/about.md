Hi! My name is <b>Abel</b>, but you can call me <b>Abel X</b>, or simply <b>X</b> as it feels easier for you.

To introduce myself and give you a little info of who I am, I created this about me page.but more I want to talk about the things I’m interested in.

Currently, I’m a BA in Psychology student at Jimma University, and also I have so many hobbies and interests—probably too many sometimes lol. But Psychology and Philosophy come first for me, and also I have interests to experience trading, programming, content creation, fitness, video editing, and in so many other things.

Fortunately, I’ve recently been trying to organize these interests into a few categories, minimize distractions, and create more focus and space for the things that matter most to me.

### My Three Main Areas of this Blog website
I’ve organized my blogs into three main categories.

### 1st. Psychology

Psychology is my main area, so it needs the most focus, attention, priority, and sometimes even the sacrifice of other interests because it is connected to my future profession. 
Because of that, 70–85% of the content here may be related to Psychology.
Personally, I have a very strong interest in understanding human behaviours like—how we think, feel, behave, and interact. That curiosity is also one of the reasons I chose Psychology as my academic field.

### 2nd. Finance World

The second area is Trading, but I’m more comfortable calling it <b>Finance</b>. I may not always give you what you might expect from the word “trading,” such as chart analysis or technical analysis.

Instead, I may focus more on fundamentals of finance world, financial concepts, and other things I find interesting and useful in this area.

### 3rd. Lifestyle
The last category is what I call Lifestyle.

This probably isn’t what you might expect when you hear the word. I’m not talking about luxury lifestyles or following every trend.
Instead, I want to share useful information about things such as technology, how-to and wiki-style guides, important lessons from my experiences, social media, and sometimes interesting things I discover from anonymous places on the internet.
So, that’s basically it.


### Why a Blog Website?

Yeah, I think this is one of the questions someone might ask.
You may have come here from one of my social media profiles, such as TikTok, Telegram, Facebook, or another platform where I have an account.

At first, I mainly tried to share my content through my Telegram channel, but I eventually found that it wasn’t ideal for either me or my readers. Social media platforms have their own limitations, and some features may require premium access or simply not provide the flexibility I need.

So, I decided to have my own blog website. 😊

Here, I have more freedom to organize my thoughts, share what I learn, and create a better place for you to explore my content. btw for my contents as main I prefer to use amharic language and sometimes English and Afan Oromo

### Other Things to Mention

As you may already know—or even if you didn’t—I enjoy taking journeys of personal growth.

Personally, that can mean reading a book, learning a language, learning something new, or simply challenging myself to do something consistently.

Recently, I’ve also tried to take some of these journeys together with a community, such as the 30 Days Journey and the 1000 Days Journey.
They don’t necessarily have the same goal for everyone; they simply share the same timespan.
For example, one person might choose to read a book in 30 days, while another might try to lose some weight or build a new habit. In the same way, during a 1000-day journey, someone might work toward earning a degree, while someone else might save enough money to buy a car, build a house for their family, or achieve another personal goal.

Whatever the goal is, I think there is something really good about having a journey and participating in one together with others.

Currently, I mainly share daily quotes since we started, but hopefully, we can do some great things together in the future.

So, check out <a href="t.me/abelzegeyieofficial">my Telegram channel</a> if you’re interested.
